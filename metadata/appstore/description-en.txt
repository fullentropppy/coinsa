Coinsa — travel expense tracker

Coinsa helps you plan budgets, track spending by location and across the whole trip, work with currencies, and get insights into your expenses.

* Trips and locations

- Create trips with a home currency and add locations with their own currencies and dates.
- View budgets, expenses, and balances for each location and the whole trip.

* Budget and expenses

Set location budgets and a separate budget for general trip expenses. Track flights, insurance, and other shared costs without assigning them to a location.

- Choose categories and subcategories: food, transport, accommodation, leisure, shopping, medicine, and miscellaneous.
- Record cash or card payments, add comments, and save expense locations on a map.
- Copy and move expenses between locations and general expenses within the same trip.
- Transfer the full positive budget balance from one location to another while keeping the total trip budget unchanged.

Exclude a prepaid or large location expense from daily spending: it reduces the available budget and stays in totals and analytics without counting toward the daily spending amount.

* Today

Follow your recommended budget, spending, and balance for today. The daily budget accounts for prepayments, other-day expenses, and amounts excluded from daily spending.

- Configure the summary budget and balance to switch to the whole location when you overspend.
- Add expenses through subcategory shortcuts based on the selected location's history.

The daily list also shows general expenses for the selected location's trip; these do not affect the location's daily budget.

* Currencies and converter

- Choose a currency for each expense, load exchange rates, and set a rate adjustment to account for fees on foreign-currency card payments.
- Refresh rates for ongoing and upcoming locations from the trip menu without changing saved expense amounts.
- Find a currency by code and name.

Use the separate converter to convert an amount and swap the conversion direction. Selected currencies and the amount are retained on the device.

* Analytics

- View total budget, spending, balance, daily averages, expense count, and the largest purchase.
- Explore daily and hourly charts, categories, and subcategories with links to individual records.
- Compare spending across trip locations.
- View expense locations on a map with nearby markers grouped together.

General expenses are included in trip totals and have their own analytics.

* Import, export, and synchronization

- Save all trips, locations, and expenses to a JSON file.
- Before importing, preview the records to be added, updated, or skipped. Records absent from the file are not deleted.

Trips, locations, and expenses sync through private iCloud storage between devices running Coinsa with the same Apple Account. Settings and converter state remain on the device.

* Settings

Choose light, dark, or system appearance, haptic feedback, and floating button placement. Configure automatic rate loading and location detection when creating an expense.

Coinsa requires no registration and does not use advertising or tracking technologies.

Requires iOS 26 or later.
